Market context

Why prices here cannot be borrowed from anywhere else.

One house, no realms

  • Forever is realmless: one AH per ruleset by faction by region = 24 markets. Yours is Normal Alliance US.
  • The RP premium is nearly gone: Timesaver week 2 (Nov 12, 21:10 UTC) puts RP at 1.11x Normal and RP-PvP at 1.22x Normal, down from 1.63x and 1.88x on Nov 7. Never import a price from another ruleset.
  • Timesaver week 2 snapshot (Nov 12, 21:10 UTC): the median material was down 41% from Nov 7 to Nov 12, 34 of 46 tracked mats fell, and listings were up 63%.
  • On Normal US, Horde lists 19% more gold than Alliance in this read. AH Ledger, Nov 13 read
  • There is no verified official Blizzard AH price API for Forever (a claimed endpoint is unverified, see Open questions). Every price here is a listing median from player addon scans, not a sale price; sold/day is an estimate.

Still a low-level economy

  • Level cap is 60, permanently, live since Nov 4. There is no phased cap increase to invest for. Beta caps (20, then 30) are the source of stale cap claims.
  • The bulk is still low: aotc.gg's census put the median level at 22, only 0.09% at 60. ForeverDB's sample (Nov 11): 33.6% at 20 to 29, 10.8% at 30 to 39, 1.5% at 40 to 49. So 10 to 30 tier mats are still the core trade for 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Gold sinks: training about 207g total 1 to 60, riding 100g at 40 and 1,000g at 60. No WoW Token, no paid boosts, GDKP banned.
  • Broad direction: 63% of items with depth were cheaper than a week earlier on the largest house, sell into demand and do not stockpile. AH Ledger gold meta, Nov 10

Open questions & coverage gaps

What the data cannot tell you yet, stated plainly.

  • Greater Mystic Essence price is unverified this run. The enchanting materials buy stays small only and carries that unverified note. Enchanting recipes are -26.6% on Nov 13, an added caution. The 32.63s figure is a Nov 8 low, not a fresh quote.
  • Leather sources conflict and are not averaged. TSM leather reagents -38.4% (Nov 13) vs. AH Ledger leather gather +19% (Nov 10 gold meta, Normal Alliance US). Different windows and methods; the brief sells leather same day under either read.
  • AH Ledger daily median differs from the headline figure. The economy page was crawled Nov 13 at 16:00 UTC, median over the last 24 hours, newest data Nov 13, 1:41 PM UTC, headline 117,420g. Its daily median table for Nov 13 prints about 126,171g for this house, a different median method; both figures stand as reported, not averaged. The gold meta is still the Nov 10 edition; the next edition is Nov 17.
  • No verified Blizzard price API, but the claim is contested. This brief's working position is that no official AH API exists and all prices are listing medians from player addon scans; most price guides re-cut the same AH Ledger dataset, so cross-site agreement is not independent confirmation. A claimed ruleset auctions endpoint is unverified, with no official Blizzard documentation found.
  • Weekday cycles: the sources flatly contradict each other. This brief prints no single weekday rule: any weekday claim is a hypothesis until roughly four weeks of Forever data on this house can measure it per category.
  • The 5% / 15% house cuts are assumed, not confirmed for Forever. Net-profit and scorecard maths uses the Classic faction (5%) and neutral (15%) cuts; Forever's own AH guides flag those rates as carried over from Classic and unconfirmed. If Forever's cuts differ, every after-costs figure moves with them.
  • TSM history is only about 9 days deep and thin categories are noisy. Plate armor, First Aid recipes, Companion Pets and Reagent Bags tiles were not readable in this run, so no value is shown for them rather than carrying stale numbers as fresh. The flips in Cloth armor (+2.2%) and Cooking recipes (+2.1%) are thin signals, watch only.
  • Tilly Sprocket's market is unspecified and her level claims conflict with census samples (median about 22). Her prices are used for direction only, never as Normal Alliance US quotes.
  • Cerulean Dye's live price is not quoted; only its recipe (Alchemy 10, 2x Frilled Lichen) and beta trading range are carried here.
  • No dependable live gold-per-hour ranking exists yet, multiple guides state this explicitly.
