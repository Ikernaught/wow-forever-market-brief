Market context

Why prices here can't be borrowed from anywhere else.

One house, no realms

  • Forever is realmless: one AH per ruleset × faction × region = 24 markets. Yours is Normal Alliance US.
  • On Nov 7, RP prices ran a median 1.63× Normal and RP-PvP 1.88× Normal; PvP ≈ Normal (1.00×). Never import a price from another ruleset. - Timesaver, Nov 7
  • PvP houses list 49% more gold than Normal (morning read); on Normal US, Horde lists 14% more gold than Alliance in the afternoon read, narrowing from 21% in the morning read. - AH Ledger, Nov 12
  • There is no verified official Blizzard AH price API for Forever (a claimed endpoint is unverified, see Open questions). Every price here is a listing median from player addon scans (AH Ledger), not a sale price; sold/day is an estimate.

Still a low-level economy

  • Level cap is 60, permanently, live since Nov 4. There is no phased cap increase to invest for. Beta caps (20, then 30) are the source of stale "cap" claims.
  • The bulk is still low: aotc.gg's census put the median level at 22, only 0.09% at 60. ForeverDB's sample (Nov 11): 33.6% at 20–29, 10.8% at 30–39, 1.5% at 40–49. So 10–30 tier mats are still the core trade for 1–2 weeks.
  • Gold sinks: training ~206–207g total 1–60, riding 100g at 40 and 1,000g at 60. No WoW Token, no paid boosts, GDKP banned.
  • Broad direction: 63% of items with depth were cheaper than a week earlier on the largest house, "sell into demand and do not stockpile." - AH Ledger gold meta, Nov 10

Open questions & coverage gaps

What the data can't tell you yet, stated plainly.

  • No verified Blizzard price API, but the claim is contested. This brief's working position (and Timesaver's, Nov 7) is that no official AH API exists and all prices are listing medians from player addon scans; most "price guides" re-cut the same AH Ledger dataset, so cross-site agreement is not independent confirmation. However: a Blizzard-forum post reports a ruleset auctions endpoint returning 404 on Nov 5, then "auctions are live as of today (Nov 6)", unofficial and undocumented, and Booty Bay Broker describes its Forever feed as hourly "regional auction data." That claim is unverified: no official Blizzard documentation was found. Until it's checked directly and dual-sourced against AH Ledger scans, treat it as an open question, not a fact, and treat any source that states either side flatly with care.
  • Weekday cycles: the sources flatly contradict each other. Booty Bay Broker says materials bottom out over the weekend and peak Tuesday/Wednesday; Almar's Classic guide says buy Mondays/Tuesdays and sell Friday/Saturday; a Classic Era flipping guide says buy midweek and sell into the Tuesday/Wednesday raid rush. These reconcile only by category: consumables peak into raid nights, general goods with weekend population, raw mats trough when weekend farmers dump. This brief prints no single weekday rule: any weekday claim is a hypothesis until roughly four weeks of Forever data on this house can measure it per category.
  • The 5% / 15% house cuts are assumed, not confirmed for Forever. Net-profit and scorecard maths uses the Classic faction (5%) and neutral (15%) cuts; Forever's own AH guides flag those rates as carried over from Classic and unconfirmed. If Forever's cuts differ, every after-costs figure moves with them.
  • TSM history is only 8 days deep and TSM doesn't publish item-level Forever medians on the pages read, category percentages are verified live; item-level TSM prices for your house were not extracted.
  • Totals disagree: Ragetusk (repackaged AH Ledger data) showed 96,041g / 171,564 auctions for Normal Alliance US vs. AH Ledger's own morning read of 99,873g / 224,103, different windows/methods. AH Ledger is the primary source here.
  • Tilly Sprocket's market is unspecified and her level claims ("most in 30s–40s") conflict with census samples (median ~22). Her prices are used for direction only, never as Normal Alliance US quotes.
  • Ruins of Lordaeron quest levels (21–22) are beta data from Wowhead Forever's beta database (checked Sep 27); no post-launch re-check was found. The official dungeon range, 15–20, is solid.
  • Cerulean Dye's live price is not quoted, AH Ledger tracks it per market, but the displayed unit formatting was ambiguous. Only its recipe (Alchemy 10, 2× Frilled Lichen) and beta trading range (1.5–6g) are carried here.
  • No dependable live gold-per-hour ranking exists yet, multiple guides state this explicitly. wowforeverguides.com has no AH price tracker; method.gg and foreverwisp.com have no economy coverage found (absence from search, not an exhaustive crawl).
  • Stale claim still circulating: beta-era pages say TSM has no Forever build. Superseded, TSM 4.15 supports Forever from launch day. Auctionator also has a Forever build.
