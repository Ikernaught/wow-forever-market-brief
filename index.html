Market context

Why prices here cannot be borrowed from anywhere else.

One house, no realms

  • Forever is realmless: one AH per ruleset by faction by region = 24 markets. Yours is Normal Alliance US.
  • The RP premium is nearly gone: Timesaver week 2 (Nov 12, 21:10 UTC) puts RP at 1.11x Normal and RP-PvP at 1.22x Normal, down from 1.63x and 1.88x on Nov 7. Never import a price from another ruleset.
  • Timesaver week 2 snapshot (Nov 12, 21:10 UTC): the median material was down 41% from Nov 7 to Nov 12, 34 of 46 tracked mats fell, and listings were up 63%.
  • PvP houses list 67% more gold than Normal houses. On Normal US, Horde lists 10% more gold than Alliance. AH Ledger, Nov 15 read
  • There is no verified official Blizzard AH price API for Forever (a claimed endpoint is unverified, see Open questions). Every price here is a listing median from player addon scans, not a sale price; sold/day is an estimate.

Still a low-level economy

  • Level cap is 60, permanently, live since Nov 4. There is no phased cap increase to invest for. Beta caps (20, then 30) are the source of stale cap claims.
  • The bulk is still low: aotc.gg's census put the median level at 22, only 0.09% at 60. ForeverDB's sample (Nov 11): 33.6% at 20 to 29, 10.8% at 30 to 39, 1.5% at 40 to 49. So 10 to 30 tier mats are still the core trade for 1 to 2 weeks.
  • Gold sinks: training about 207g total 1 to 60, riding 100g at 40 and 1,000g at 60, dual spec 50g at 40. No WoW Token, no paid boosts, GDKP banned.
  • Broad direction: 63% of items with depth were cheaper than a week earlier on the largest house, sell into demand and do not stockpile. AH Ledger gold meta, Nov 10
